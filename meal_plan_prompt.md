## Weight-Loss Guidelines

- Aim for roughly **½ plate vegetables, ¼ protein, and ¼ whole-grain/starchy foods** at lunch and dinner.
- Keep cooking oil to approximately **3–4 teaspoons per person per day**, including oil used in cooking.
- Minimize sweets, biscuits, fried snacks, bakery foods, sugary drinks, alcohol, and refined-flour foods.
- Choose whole fruit instead of fruit juice.
- Include a protein source at each meal.
- If hungry between meals, choose vegetables, buttermilk, a small portion of nuts, or fruit rather than biscuits/snacks.
- If medically appropriate, aim for **20–30 minutes of walking most days**.

> **Note:** This is a general healthy eating plan, not a personalized medical diet. If there is diabetes, high blood pressure, kidney disease, thyroid disease, high cholesterol, or medication use, portions and food choices should be individualized by a qualified dietitian or doctor.
