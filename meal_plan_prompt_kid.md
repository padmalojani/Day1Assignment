## Badminton Practice Nutrition

### Before Practice
- 60–90 minutes before practice: banana + milk, fruit + nuts, or 2 small idlis/dosa.
- Avoid heavy, fried foods immediately before playing.

### After Practice
Choose one:
- Milk + banana
- Curd/yogurt + fruit
- Paneer sandwich
- Small dosa/idli + milk

### Hydration
- Encourage regular water intake throughout the day.
- Give additional water before, during and after badminton practice.
- Sugary soft drinks and energy drinks should be avoided.

### General Guidelines
- Include vegetables and fruits of different colors each day.
- Use milk/curd/paneer regularly for calcium and protein.
- Include dal, beans, chickpeas, nuts and seeds on vegetarian days.
- Prefer home-cooked meals and minimize packaged snacks.
- Portion sizes should be adjusted according to the child's appetite, growth and badminton training duration.
- Avoid restrictive weight-loss diets for children unless specifically advised by a pediatrician or qualified dietitian.
